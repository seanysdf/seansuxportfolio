About Me

I'm a UX designer passionate about creating digital experiences that are both beautiful and accessible. Currently conducting accessibility testing for the OneBusAway app at the University of Washington, I focus on ensuring transit information is accessible to all users, regardless of ability.

My approach combines thorough accessibility audits, user testing with assistive technologies, and WCAG compliance validation to ensure every user can navigate transit systems confidently. I believe great design should work for everyone, not just some people.

When I'm not testing and improving accessibility, you'll find me staying current with accessibility standards, exploring new design tools, and contributing to the UX community.

My Design Process

A structured approach to solving design challenges through research, iteration, and validation.

1

Discover

Understanding user needs through research, interviews, and competitive analysis to identify core problems.

2

Define

Synthesizing research into actionable insights, user personas, and clear problem statements.

3

Ideate

Exploring multiple solutions through sketching, wireframing, and collaborative brainstorming sessions.

4

Design

Creating high-fidelity prototypes with attention to visual design, interaction patterns, and accessibility.

5

Test

Validating designs through usability testing and gathering feedback for iterative improvements.

6

Iterate

Refining designs based on user feedback and testing results to optimize the user experience.
